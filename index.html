Traffic Light Controller

This traffic light controller uses a memory-based circuit built entirely from NAND gates to manage the light sequence. The D flip-flops act as storage units that remember the current color, while a network of logic gates calculates which light should turn on next. Each pulse of a clock signal pushes the system to the next step in the cycle, ensuring a smooth and repeatable transition from green to yellow to red.
